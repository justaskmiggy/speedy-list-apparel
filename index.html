How it works

Four steps. About a minute.

  1. 1

    Snap

    Front, back, brand tag, size/care tag and any flaws. Up to 3 detail shots.

  2. 2

    AI reads it

    Brand, size, fabric, era, condition and flaws, plus what to measure.

  3. 3

    Speedy price

    V = (R × C) + B: original retail × condition, plus a bonus for in-demand brands. A starting point you can change.

  4. 4

    Copy & post

    Each site gets its own version: Etsy tags, eBay item specifics, Depop hashtags, Shopify CSV.
